Won't connect — what to check

This connects with a direct link to Twitch's own chat servers — no outside library required, so it works the moment the page loads. If a channel still won't connect: double-check the spelling of the username (not display name — the exact login, lowercase, no spaces), confirm the channel exists and isn't banned/suspended, and check that your network doesn't block WebSocket connections (some school/corporate networks do — try a phone hotspot to confirm). Press F12 → Console for the exact error if it keeps failing.
